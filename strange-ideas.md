# Strange Ideas & Cognitive Discoveries

## 🧠 Neuro-Hacks & Biological Phenomena
- **Tachypsychia (Time Dilation):** The experience of time slowing down during crisis. Not a physical slow-down, but an increase in memory recording density via the amygdala.
- **The Colorless Void:** Loss of color during extreme stress due to the brain prioritizing motion/contrast (rods) over color (cones) to optimize survival.
- **The "Adrenaline Dump":** The physiological crash after a hyper-focused state, characterized by tremors and extreme fatigue.
- **The Extended Mind (External RAM):** Using physical paper/drawings to offload working memory, turning the environment into a cognitive prosthetic.
- **Panoramic Vision:** Using long-distance gazing to trigger the parasympathetic nervous system and lower cortisol.

## 🐒 Evolutionary Theories (The Canopy Model)
- **The Hypergraph Brain:** The theory that human intelligence is an extension of arboreal navigation. We don't see objects; we see a weighted hypergraph of possibilities (affordances), just as a monkey sees branches.
- **The Snake Detection Module:** The evolutionary hard-wiring in the visual cortex designed to detect serpentine patterns instantly.
- **Physical Intelligence:** The idea that jumping between branches in childhood physically expands the brain's ability to navigate abstract, multi-dimensional problems in adulthood.
